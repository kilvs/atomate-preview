How ATOmate works

Capture & identify

Every ATO document is captured overnight and matched to the right client using their ABN or TFN.

ATO documents are cross-checked against ATO and practice management data.

TFNs are automatically redacted. Documents with a TFN-based PRN are sent as PIN-protected PDFs.

Documents that meet your rules are approved. Flagged items are sent for review.

Send client-ready communications automatically or on your own terms. Get a full audit trail upon filing.
