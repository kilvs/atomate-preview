## The idea
ATOmate's story is time: documents arrive at night and are done by morning. The page is a night that turns into a morning.
- Hero: deep navy (#021F35 → #03466B), dawn glow top-right, the arrow as one 2px white stroke running along the hero's base then climbing at 30° to an orange head near the top-right; "Overnight" at the bottom-left, "Next-day approval" in orange near the head. White lockup in the nav, white CTA. H1 4.5rem white with "90%" in orange-300.
- Stats: one giant numeral row (4rem) under the hero on the same navy — 2,000+ white, 90% orange, lorem 00% dimmed.
- Integrations (day): pill tabs, logos on a soft white shelf of elevated cards.
- Comparison: night beside day — a navy card (Without, muted ✕) and a white card (With, blue ✓). *(The gradient bridge and the 7rem orange "90%" in the header were removed at merge.)*
- How it works: *(alternate's staircase not used — the original draft's frame is the accepted one.)*
- Overnight processing: night again (navy) with the queue UI.
- Review less / filing: elevated white cards on a light stage, one navy card as emphasis.
- PAYG: dawn gradient (navy → blue) with the white mark ghosted. *(Glass/blur effect removed at merge.)*
- Better informed: pull quotes at 2.25rem beside UI.
- Messaging: email as paper on a navy panel with 30° ghost lines; channels as pills.
- Security: *(pills in the header replaced by the quiet credentials line after review)*; two UI cards of equal height.
- Testimonials: one large navy quote card + two small.
- CTA (morning): white → blue-100 gradient with the full-colour mark standing at 14% at the right.

## System differences from the ledger-grid draft it replaced
No ruled frames; cards are 1.5rem radius, elevated (navy-tinted shadow), on tinted stages. Type is larger and more editorial (h2 3rem, lead 1.375rem). Orange = outcome, used for the arrowhead, the 90% figures, the active step icon and the comparison arrow. Buttons 0.5rem.
